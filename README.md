### 💼 What I've Worked On

**💳 Payments & reconciliation** · *Education platform, 2026*<br>
The LMS order, payment and reconciliation flow. VNPay, VietQR and the office POS terminals confirm payments through webhooks, with one merchant account per legal entity. Each transaction updates the order, syncs to HubSpot and sends a notification. Cancelling an order also cancels it on the other systems, contract included.<br>
`94% paid online` · `99.9% gateway success` · `100% orders synced to HubSpot`

**📝 E-contracts** · *Education platform, 2026*<br>
I moved the e-signature provider from FPT to EFY while the system was live. I rewrote the integration layer, ran both providers side by side for a month, then switched FPT off, with no downtime. HubSpot data fills each contract automatically, duplicates are blocked, and the system recovers when the provider reports a network error even though the contract was actually created.<br>
`3,936 contracts in 9 months` · `~215,000 manual entries removed` · `~980 hours saved`

**🧰 Internal tools** · *Education platform, 2026*<br>
Small tools to cut manual work: copying HubSpot properties and pipelines from production to dev, viewing a record's raw property history, letting BAs check and retry a failed contract without asking a dev, and tracking error rates for the contract flow.

**💬 Real-time social app** · *Social startup, 2025*<br>
Microservices for a social app, at a startup of about ten people with two backend developers. I was the main one and joined from the start: Kafka, WebSocket and MQTT for real-time features, Redis and Elasticsearch for queries, PostgreSQL and MongoDB schemas.

**🎮 Telegram mini apps** · *Telegram games, 2023 – 2025*<br>
Telegram mini app games plus a few web apps. My first job, which I started while still at university, going from part-time to almost full-time. This is where I learned to work with a team and put APIs together.
